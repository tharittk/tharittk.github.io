<div style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px;">
  <em> "Knowing facts is different from having an understanding" </em>
</div>
<br>
These are things I may have heard multiple times but they only "clicked" some time later.

1. Take a simple idea and take it seriously
  <br>
    *When you figure out how the world work in one or more aspect, go all-in.*
2. Trust is the most expensive currency
  <br>
    *Like reputation, it takes 20 years to build and 5 minutes to destroy it.*
3. Mistake is a blessing
  <br>
    *At the moment of pain, you can't possibly see how this is going to be a blessing. Only when looking backward, it becomes clear - almost like a prerequisite for the next stage of life.*
4. Men are reasoning rather than reasonable animals
  <br>
    *Alexander Hamilton said it too well.*
5. You can't make money by trying to make it.
  <br>
    *Same thing goes with credit. You cannot claim the credit and expect people to give it. In fact, when you don't, people tend to give you even more.*
6. Humans are evasive to cloning. But lots of good ideas have been figured out.
  <br>
    *Charlie Munger once said Sam Walton practially invented nothing new. Charlie believed he himself did not too.*
7. Revolution in a hurry leads good men into excess
  <br>
    *Hamilton again. Charlie's version probably is: if you mix raisin with turds. That's still turd.*
8. The longer communication pipeline is, the more information is lost
  <br>
    *Compound this with incentives, you will get a pretty dysfunctional organization*
9. Time is the ultimate stress test
  <br>
    *A hotshot, fast money, a saint you once worship, how many are still there? This is from Li Lu: If you don't have intellectual honesty, you will be tested.*
10. If you wrestle with the pig, both of you will get muddy but the pig will enjoy it.
  <br>
    *There're people who love drama and ugly fight.*
11. The teacher will show up once the student is ready.
  <br>
    *There're things you can't grasp until you accumulate certain level of life experience.*
12. Experience is what you get when you didn't get what you want
  <br>
    *That's from Howard Marks.*
13. Randomness causes immunity. Anti-fragility is tolerance, or even thrive, under extreme randomness.
  <br>
    *From Nassim Taleb*
14. There's no adult in the room. Everybody is trying to figure out themselves.
  <br>
    *This is from Peter Thiel. I cannot remember when and where. Your CEO, your VP, your manager are dealing with things they probably never encounter in their life.*
15. Belief comes from capability. Champions behave like one before become one.
  <br>
    *No need to say it futher*
16. Inversion and choice elimination is easier
  <br>
    *What will happen in the future ? Too hard. How about something that will never change? that is easier.*
17. Societal anomaly, extreme phenomena can usually be explained through the confluence of simple ideas.
  <br>
    *Munger's lollapalooza*
18. The goal is not about getting rich. It is staying sane.
  <br>
    *Once you see enough unhappy rich people and family, you will get it*
19. It never occurs to me that making the most money is the goal in life
  <br>
    *Howard Marks said it. My brother said it.*
20. It is not greed that drives the world. It's envy.
  <br>
    *Nothing can trouble the mind of a smart man more than seeing his idiot friend gets rich. Warren Buffett jokingly said Adam Smith was wrong to say that greed is what drives the world.*
