Zeno went to see the Oracle of Delphi and asked what's *the secret to life*. The cryptic answer was:
<br>
<div style="text-align: center; font-size: 18px;">
  <em> "You will become wise once you start to have conversations with the dead." </em>
</div>
<br>

1. Take a simple idea and take it seriously
  <br>
    *When you figure out how the world works in one or more aspects, go all-in.*
2. Trust is the most expensive currency
  <br>
    *Like reputation, it takes 20 years to build and 5 minutes to destroy it.*
3. Mistake is a blessing
  <br>
    *At the moment of pain, you can't possibly see how this is going to be a blessing. Only when looking backward does it become clear - almost like a prerequisite for the next stage of life.*
4. Men are reasoning rather than reasonable animals
  <br>
    *Alexander Hamilton said it too well.*
5. You can't make money by trying to make it.
  <br>
    *Same thing goes with credit. You cannot claim the credit and expect people to give it. In fact, when you don't, people tend to give you even more.*
6. Humans are averse to cloning. But lots of good ideas have been figured out.
  <br>
    *Charlie Munger once said Sam Walton practically invented nothing new. Charlie believed he himself did not either.*
7. Revolution in a hurry leads good men into excess
  <br>
    *Hamilton again. Charlie's version probably is: if you mix raisins with turds, that's still a turd.*
8. The longer the communication pipeline is, the more information is lost
  <br>
    *Compound this with incentives, and you will get a pretty dysfunctional organization.*
9. Time is the ultimate stress test
  <br>
    *A hotshot, fast money, a saint you once worship - how many are still there? This is from Li Lu: If you don't have intellectual honesty, you will be tested.*
10. If you wrestle with the pig, both of you will get muddy but the pig will enjoy it.
  <br>
    *There're people who love drama and ugly fights.*
11. The teacher will show up once the student is ready.
  <br>
    *There're things you can't grasp until you accumulate a certain level of life experience.*
12. Experience is what you get when you didn't get what you want
  <br>
    *That's from Howard Marks.*
13. Randomness causes immunity. Anti-fragility is tolerance, or even thriving, under extreme randomness.
  <br>
    *From Nassim Taleb*
14. There's no adult in the room. Everybody is trying to figure out themselves.
  <br>
    *This is from Peter Thiel. I cannot remember when and where. Your CEO, your VP, and your manager are dealing with things they probably never encountered in their lives.*
15. Belief comes from capability. Champions behave like one before becoming one.
  <br>
    *No need to say it further.*
16. Inversion and choice elimination is easier
  <br>
    *What will happen in the future? Too hard. How about something that will never change? That is easier.*
17. Societal anomalies and extreme phenomena can usually be explained through the confluence of simple ideas.
  <br>
    *Munger's lollapalooza*
18. The goal is not about getting rich. It is staying sane.
  <br>
    *Once you see enough unhappy rich people and families, you will get it.*
19. It never occurs to me that making the most money is the goal in life
  <br>
    *Howard Marks said it. My brother said it.*
20. It is not greed that drives the world. It's envy.
  <br>
    *Nothing can trouble the mind of a smart man more than seeing his idiot friend get rich. Warren Buffett jokingly said Adam Smith was wrong to say that greed is what drives the world.*
21. The Golden Day never comes. It only exists in the past.
   <br>
    *You never know whether you're in one or not. You can only tell when you look back.*
22. Love is something you give before you get it back.
   <br>
    *It is not guaranteed to get it back. Actually, you should not expect it. Giving love freely already already serves a 
    joyful purpose.*
23. It is harder to be kind than be clever.
    <br>
    *Bezos said it well enough*
24. It's unfortunate that this happens to me. No, It is fortunate. It's the opportunity to excercise what you'd practice. It's an opportunity to learn something
    <br>
    *Taken from random places in Stoic readings*
25. Friendship takes effort to maintain and does not happen automatically like when you were young.
    <br>
    *Hope I didn't recognize this too late*
26. A well-groomed, smart person with good interpersonal skills is not interesting. If you have those traits, it's easy for you to attain the position of power. You should look at those who're no good. Those you can't believe to rise. That's real politics.
    <br>
    *It brought me a good laugh when I heard it first time. But it's very very true*
27. The future is going to come whether you like it or not. So you might as well be excited about it.
    <br>
    *Speaking of A.I. and interesting time when I nearly graduate the CS program*
28. Still hurted but didn't bother that much (anymore)
    <br>
    Snap of the same little breaks
